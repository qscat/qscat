name=QGIS Shoreline Change Analysis Tool

description=A plugin for Shoreline Change Analysis (SCA).

about=QSCAT offers the following features:

    * Shoreline Change: computes shoreline change envelope (SCE), net shoreline movement (NSM), end-point rate (EPR), linear regression rate (LRR), and weighted linear regression rate (WLR).

    * Area Change: calculates the length and area of accreting, stable, and eroding coastal segments.

    * Forecasting: predicts future shoreline positions, including uncertainty areas.

    * Automator: automates repetitive manual tasks.

    * Visualization: shoreline change, area change, and forecasting results are visualized on the map for easy interpretation.

    * Summary Reports: generates comprehensive summary reports of the analysis results.

    For the changelog, please visit https://github.com/qscat/qscat/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
